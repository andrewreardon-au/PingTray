# PingTray privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

PingTray does not collect or send any personal information. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

**What it does on the network.** The only traffic PingTray creates is the test it runs against the host you choose (1.1.1.1 unless you change it): a ping, a TCP connect or a DNS query, depending on the method you pick. If you enter a host name, your computer looks it up through its normal DNS setup. Nothing is sent to the author or to anyone else.

**What it keeps on your computer.** Your Settings (host, method, port, interval and colour limits) are saved in a small `settings.ini` file, next to the exe in the zip version or in the app's private storage in the Microsoft Store version. The Store version's copy is removed when you uninstall. Nothing else is saved, and no history of readings is kept.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can go to the project's issue tracker: https://github.com/andrewreardon-au/PingTray/issues

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
